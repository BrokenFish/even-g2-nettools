# Net Tools – Privacy Policy

_Effective date: November 11, 2026_

This privacy policy explains how the Net Tools plug-in for Even Realities G2 glasses ("the app") handles information. The app is developed and maintained by an independent developer ("we") and is not affiliated with Even Realities.

## Summary

Net Tools does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## What the app processes

- **Calculator input.** When you use the subnet calculator, the address you type (for example `192.168.1.10/26`) is processed on your phone to calculate the result. The last address you entered is saved in the app's local storage on your device, so it is still there the next time you open the app. It never leaves your device.
- **Glasses input.** Taps and scroll gestures from the glasses or ring are used only to navigate the app's screens. They are not recorded or stored.

## What the app does not do

- It does not request network access and makes no network requests.
- It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
- It does not access your microphone, camera, photos, location, contacts or account information.
- It does not create user accounts.

## Data stored on your device

The only data stored is the last calculator input described above. You can remove it by clearing the input and calculating again, or by uninstalling the app.

## Third parties

The app runs inside the Even Realities app. Your use of the Even Realities app and glasses is covered by Even Realities' own privacy policy. We receive no data from Even Realities about you or your use of Net Tools.

## Children

The app does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same location with a new effective date.
