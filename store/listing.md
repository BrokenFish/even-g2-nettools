## App description

Net Tools puts the networking reference you keep looking up right in front of your eyes.

SUBNETS
Browse every subnet from /32 to /8, smallest first. Open any prefix to see its mask, wildcard, usable hosts, block size and binary mask, plus a worked example with network address, first and last host, broadcast and next subnet. A third page lists how the block splits its parent /24, /16 or /8. Scroll past the last page to step to the next prefix.

CHEAT SHEETS
- Mask octets and block sizes
- Wildcard masks for ACLs and OSPF
- Private and special IPv4 ranges (RFC 1918, CGNAT, link-local, TEST-NET)
- Classful ranges
- Common TCP/UDP ports
- IPv6 address types and prefixes
- OSI model
- Troubleshooting commands for Linux, macOS and Windows
- MTU, header sizes and VLANs

CALCULATOR
Type an address such as 192.168.1.10/26 on your phone and the result is shown on the glasses. You can also split a network into smaller subnets.

Works fully offline. No account, no tracking.
